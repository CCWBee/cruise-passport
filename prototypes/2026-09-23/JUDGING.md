### Why

Under the guest lens the question is whether you would want it on your phone for fifteen days and whether it makes you want the next medal. Only C does both. Its medals on the velvet tray and in the case look worth earning, with the ringed blank as the clearest nudge to the next one. The room is fun to be in, it is easy on the eyes in a bar at night (where most drinks get logged), and Ship tells you which bar is open. A is the easiest to use and the best at noon, and it has the best sheet. But away from Home it is today's cream list app with a capsule on it, and at 23:00 it is a white torch, so it misses Charles's 'revolution not tweak' and 'not fun'. B is the only one that adapts to both glare and night, and that is the axis Charles named, so it is worth taking. But the liner and tide line behind every list, the stacked platters and the wordiest Home make it the hardest to read at a glance with a drink in the other hand. C's defects (no Tried at medium, an unlabelled '+', dark at noon) can each be fixed by borrowing from A and B. A's weakness is that four screens are unchanged, and B's is its whole backdrop concept for lists. Neither is a quick fix.

### Why

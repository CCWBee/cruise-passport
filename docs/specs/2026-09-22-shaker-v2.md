## What is wrong with the shipped one

Filmed frame by frame with `tools/qa/film.mjs` (contact sheet at
`tools/qa/shots-film/shake-now/sheet.png`):

1. **It reads as a pedal bin.** The tin is squat and wide (a 132-wide body under a domed lid), so
   the silhouette is a kitchen bin, not a cocktail shaker. A cobbler shaker is tall and narrow: a
   body that tapers to its base, a strainer top that narrows to a neck, and a small cap.
2. **The shake is a wobble.** It rocks ±3° to ±12° about a low pivot with 2 to 3px of jitter. A
   shaker is shaken: the motion is travel, up and down along a diagonal over the shoulder, tens of
   pixels, the tin tilting as it goes, getting faster and harder.
3. **The lid hinges open like a bin lid.** The brief is the top coming off and the prize popping
   out, the way a loot crate opens: pressure, then release.
4. **The prize reads as the numeral 7.** It is a 44px disc holding `IconDrinks` at 20px, and that
   glyph draws only one side of the glass (it is the brand's funnel mark, which reads correctly at
   24px beside the word Drinks and nowhere else). The prize says nothing about the drink.
5. **There is no build-up into the pop.** The 300ms hold is a still tin. Nothing says the pop is
   coming, so it arrives as a cut rather than a release.
6. **The sheet grew twice mid-moment**, lifting the drawing up the screen as the card and then
   "Shake again" arrived. Fixed in the harness before this round (below); no variant may bring it
   back.
7. **The card fades in as one block**, so the name, the place and the reason land as a single grey
   smear rather than being read in order.

## What is settled and stays

- The pick (`pick.ts`, tested), the sound engine (`rattle.ts`), the haptics (`haptic('shake')` at
  the press, `haptic('success')` at the landing), the live region, the still-on-return behaviour,
  and the sheet's structure: title, meta line, the shaker, the answer slot, the one coral button,
  the quiet actions. A variant changes the drawing and the motion, not the sheet.
- The harness, already in place: `src/features/shake/variants/index.ts` defines `ShakerVariant`;
  `?shaker=a|b|c` on a QA load picks a variant; the sheet reads every timing from it
  (`shakeMs`, `popMs`, `landMs`, `closeMs`); `startRattle(quiet, shakeMs)` stretches the dice to
  the variant's shake and `reveal(landMs)` puts the thock on its landing. The answer has a held
  slot (`.shake-slot`) and "Shake again" keeps its line before a reveal, so the sheet is one height
  shut, shaking and open.
- The constitution. Colour: `--ink` stroke on `--cream` fill, as the icon set and the current
  drawing do; white at low opacity is allowed as the light on steel (the glass chrome's specular
  is the precedent) and nothing else is: no coral, gold, mint or friend hue in the drawing, because
  each of those means something elsewhere (DESIGN.md Colour). No emoji, no pill, no dot, no box or
  panel around the shaker, no glass on it. 2D: SVG and CSS. It is finite, it is started by the
  guest, nothing loops and nothing moves at rest.
- Motion craft, from Emil Kowalski's and Jakub Krehel's skills (`emilkowalski/skills`
  `emil-design-eng`, `animate`, `review-animations/STANDARDS.md`; `jakubkrehel/skills`
  `better-ui`), inside the constitution's own rules:
  - animate `transform` and `opacity` only (`clip-path` allowed); never `scale(0)`: an entrance
    starts at 0.9 or above with opacity;
  - anticipation before a move (a small wind-up the opposite way), follow-through after it (one
    overshoot and a settle, never a bounce loop: the two written exceptions in DESIGN.md Motion
    stand as the pattern);
  - entrances on a strong ease-out: `--e-out` is `cubic-bezier(.22, .9, .32, 1)`, within a hair
    of Kowalski's `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`; use the token. Travel on screen, the shake
    itself, may set per-keyframe timing functions inside its keyframes (the physics of a hand
    reversing direction), and each such curve is a token or is listed in
    `tools/qa/design-allow.txt` with its reason;
  - exits softer and faster than entrances (the reverse before Shake again stays about 240ms);
  - the card's three lines stagger: name, then place, then reason, 60 to 100ms apart, each from
    opacity 0, `translateY(8px)` and `blur(4px)` to rest;
  - reduced motion is gentler, not nothing: no travel, no rotation, no sound; the prize and the
    card fade to their end state over `var(--t-panel)` and the reveal still lands.
- Budget. Press to prize landed within 3.0s; card fully in within 3.4s. The shake itself at least
  1.4s, because the build-up is the point: this is the rare, guest-started, delight tier.
- Geometry. The stage (the `Shaker`'s own box) is one fixed size in every phase. Nothing drawn may
  paint over the sheet title and meta above it or the answer slot below it; whatever flies must
  stay inside the stage or leave it by fading. At 390×844 the sheet's title, stage, slot, button and
  quiet actions all fit without the sheet scrolling: measure it.

## Charles's reference, 22 September

He sent a photograph of two polished steel cobbler shakers: "More what I conceptualise a cocktail
shaker looking like". The photograph is a manufacturer's product shot and the repo is public, so it
is not committed; a copy for this round is at `.qa/reference/shaker-reference.jpg` (gitignored).
Open it before drawing anything. What it shows, and what every drawing in this round follows:

- **Three pieces and a band, top to bottom.** A short cylindrical **cap** with three or four
  horizontal grooves round its lower half, sitting on a short neck; a smooth convex **dome**, the
  strainer top, swelling like a bell from the neck to its widest point; a **band** where the
  strainer's skirt overlaps the rolled rim of the body (two close parallel rings, the widest part of
  the whole shaker); the **body**, a tall cone tapering from the band to a narrower base with a
  slightly rounded foot.
- **Proportions**, measured off the photograph as fractions of the total height: cap 9%, dome 21%,
  band 7%, body 63%. The whole shaker is about 2.5 times as tall as it is wide. The cap is about 40%
  of the band's width, the neck about 45%, and the base about 72% of the body's top width. It is
  tall and slender: nothing like the squat tin that shipped.
- **Material.** Polished steel: strong vertical highlights running the length of the dome and the
  body, left of centre. In this app's idiom that is the `--ink` stroke on the `--cream` fill with
  one or two vertical light strips in white at low opacity, and, if a shade is wanted, `--line`
  (ink at 12%) as the only shade: light and shade on steel, nothing else coloured.
- **Engraving.** The photographed shakers carry fine line-art engraving round the lower body
  (flowers on one, mountains on the other). A few engraved lines of sea round the lower body, in
  `--ink-3` at a hairline, would put the app's own sea on the tin; it is optional, a builder may
  leave it off, and the judges weigh whether it earns its place.

## The prize: item first, then its card

In every variant the prize tells the guest the kind of drink before the card names it: a glass
drawn for the drink's category, big enough to read (the glass itself at least 40px tall), in the
shaker's own stroke idiom, never `IconDrinks`. `drink` reaches the `Shaker` at the pop, so the
glass can be right. The families, from `CATEGORIES` in `src/data/model.ts`:

| Category | Glass |
| --- | --- |
| Martini, Classic, Signature, Dessert, and any category not listed (the two Princess brand names) | a stemmed V-shaped cocktail glass, both sides of the bowl drawn |
| Margarita | a margarita glass (stepped bowl) |
| Wine, Spritz | a wine glass (round bowl on a stem) |
| Beer | a pint glass |
| Coffee | a cup on a saucer |
| Frozen | a hurricane glass with a straw |
| Mocktail | a highball with a straw |

Each glass is drawn once, as its own component inside the variant's folder; the winner's set is
kept when it is folded in.

## Three angles, one builder each

Take your angle to its best version; do not converge on the others'. Each is judged on film.

**A. The cobbler, cap off.** The iconic three-piece cobbler shaker, tall and narrow, with a steel
light down the body. The shake is two-handed travel on a diagonal, gentle, harder, violent, the
cap chattering against the strainer as it goes. The held beat is pressure: the tin trembles and the
cap lifts a couple of pixels. Then the cap pops straight up and tumbles away, fading as it leaves
the stage, and the prize glass rises out of the strainer's mouth on one overshoot and hangs, with a
short burst of ink strokes around it for a single frame's worth of release (drawn, finite, gone in
under 300ms).

**B. The top comes off.** The same cobbler, and the brief taken literally: the whole top (dome and
cap together, as one piece) is the crate's lid. The held beat is the seal straining: the band
flexes, the top lifts a hair and settles back, twice, faster the second time. Then the top blasts
off the body as one piece, rising and tilting away and fading as it leaves the stage, and the prize
glass rises out of the body's wide mouth on one overshoot and hangs. (Replaces the Boston tins of
the first draft: Charles's reference is a cobbler, so every variant is one.)

**C. The dice roll.** The brief's dice. A cobbler shaker; the shake rattles the dice audibly and
visibly (the tin's motion carries it); at the pop the cap comes off and a die tumbles out onto a
drawn surface line, rolling two or three decelerating quarter turns and landing face up with the
glass for the drink's category drawn on its face: the 8-ball's answer, on a die. The card follows
under it.

## What each builder may touch

Only `src/features/shake/variants/<letter>/` (its `index.tsx`, its `<letter>.css`, and any `.tsx`
it adds there). Nothing else in the repo, including the shared sheet, `rattle.ts`, `Shaker.tsx`,
`shake.css`, the tokens and `Icon.tsx`. No commits: the round is judged on the working tree and the
winner is folded in by a later pass. Scope every CSS rule under `.shaker-<letter>` or
`.shake[data-shaker="<letter>"]`, so no variant can move another. `ShakerProps` is `{ phase,
still, drink }`; `phase` is `'idle' | 'shaking' | 'opening' | 'revealed' | 'closing'`, and
'shaking' lasts `popMs` (the shake and the held beat), 'opening' lasts `landMs`, 'closing' lasts
`closeMs`. `still` means come back at the end state with no animation (see `shake.css`'s
`is-still` block for why).

## How to check your own work

The dev server on 127.0.0.1:5173 is already running and shared; never start another on 5173 and
never restart it. Two other builders are editing their own folders at the same time, so a hot
update can reset a take: if a film shows the sheet reloading, film it again.

```bash
npx tsc -p tsconfig.app.json --noEmit
SHOTS_DIR=shots-v2-<letter> node tools/qa/film.mjs <letter> "?shaker=<letter>" --click ".shake-open" --press ".shake-go" --record 3600 --every 120 --crop ".shake-body" --cols 8
SHOTS_DIR=shots-v2-<letter> node tools/qa/film.mjs <letter>-reduced "?shaker=<letter>" --click ".shake-open" --press ".shake-go" --record 1200 --every 100 --crop ".shake-body" --cols 6 --reduced
SHOTS_DIR=shots-v2-<letter> node tools/qa/film.mjs <letter>-again "?shaker=<letter>" --click ".shake-open" --click2 ".shake-go" --after2 4200 --press ".shake-again" --record 3900 --every 150 --crop ".shake-body" --cols 8
node tools/qa/scan.mjs src/features/shake/variants/<letter>
```

Read every `sheet.png` you make, honestly, as a guest would: does it read as a cocktail shaker at
rest; does the shake build; is there a moment of pressure before the pop; does the pop release; can
you tell what kind of drink it is before you read the name; does anything jump, clip or leave the
stage. Iterate until it does. `film.html` in the same folder plays the take at real speed and at a
quarter speed.

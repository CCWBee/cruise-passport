# Shake: the shaker picks a drink

17 September 2026. Branch `shake`, on top of the live product (`f1d9d92`). Read `docs/DESIGN.md` in
full first (Thesis, Material, Motion, States, Copy, Home under Screens, the registry), then
`CLAUDE.md`, then every file named under What exists as it stands on the branch. Anchor on symbols,
never on line numbers.

## Purpose

Charles's brief, in his words: a button that borrows the build-up and reveal of a lootbox opening,
but the object is a cocktail shaker with dice in it and the reveal is 8-ball style, a suggestion
surfacing; you press, it shakes, the anticipation, the noise, the haptics, and it tells you which
drink to go and get. All 2D. The app already has the pieces: a catalogue, the taste signals behind
"For you", the drink sheet where a drink is logged, `haptic()`, `Confirm`, the `Sheet` stage.

Inside the constitution this is the app's one moment of theatre, and it earns it two ways: the pick
is genuinely useful (untried, weighted by what the guest and their crew like, never a drink they
have had), and it is silent and still until asked. The theatre lives inside a sheet, on demand,
user-triggered, finite. Home stays an instrument.

## Design placement

**Home, the entry point.** One `button.row.pressable.shake-open` at the foot of the "For you"
section, after the shelf, in its own wrapper `div`, no chevron, `aria-haspopup="dialog"`:

> **Shake for a drink**
> The shaker picks one you have not tried

It is the playful last item of the section that already exists to suggest drinks, so it borrows
that section's heading and adds none. When "For you" has no shelf (a new passport, no ratings, no
crew) the section still renders, heading plus this one row, because for that guest the shaker is
the most useful thing on the screen. When the active sailing has no drinks at all (a BYO sailing
with nothing added yet) the row does not render: there is nothing to shake. Ink only on Home; Log a
drink keeps the screen's coral.

**The Shake sheet** (`src/features/shake/ShakeSheet.tsx`), opened from that row. Rank order:

1. Title `Shake` (`h2.t-title.sheet-title`), one meta line (`.sheet-meta`).
2. The shaker (`src/features/shake/Shaker.tsx`): a drawn 2D cobbler shaker, cap, strainer top,
   tapered body, about 176px tall, `--ink` stroke on a `--cream` fill, centred on the ground (no
   panel: the sheet is the container and a box around the only thing present is the fourth banned
   tell). In its body, the window: a circle about 112px across filled `--ink`, the 8-ball window.
   Idle, it is dark. After a shake the answer surfaces in it. The shaker is the one dominant element.
3. Beneath the shaker, the answer's meta and reason (empty until a reveal).
4. The one filled control, `button.btn.btn-coral.btn-wide.shake-go`.
5. Quiet actions (`.quiet-action`): "Shake again" (reveal state only), and the sound toggle
   (always): "Shake quietly" when sound is on, "Shake with sound" when off.

Geometry: `--s5` (24) between modules 2 to 5, `--s3` (12) between the two quiet actions, the
shaker centred with `justify-self: center`. Radius 12 on the button, none on the shaker. Colour:
`--ink` and `--cream` for the shaker, `--ink` for the window's liquid, `--cream` for the surfaced
answer text (it is cream on ink: check 4.5:1, `--cream` on `--ink` is well over), `--coral-ink` on
the button only. No glass on the shaker: it sits in the content layer.

The four banned tells: no pill or dot anywhere; no rule under the title or along the shaker; no
emoji (the shaker is drawn SVG); one dominant element.

## Behaviour

Copy verbatim. British English, sentence case, no em dashes, dry.

### Idle

Meta line: **The shaker picks one you have not tried.** The window is dark. Button: **Shake**.
Beneath it the sound toggle. No reason line, no meta beneath the shaker.

### Press (the build-up, about 2.1 seconds)

1. `haptic('tap')`. The button becomes `disabled` for the duration (it renders as the ghost the
   polish pass gave disabled controls) and reads **Shaking**.
2. The shaker shakes in three phases, one CSS animation on the shaker's root, `--e-out` throughout:
   gentle (0 to 0.6s, rotate ±3°), harder (0.6 to 1.3s, rotate ±8° with 2px x and y jitter),
   violent (1.3 to 1.8s, rotate ±12°, 3px jitter), then a hard stop upright at 1.8s and a **hold of
   300ms** with the window still dark: the anticipation beat a lootbox takes before it opens. Nothing
   else on the sheet moves.
3. Sound, from `src/features/shake/rattle.ts`, WebAudio, synthesised, no asset: two dice in a metal
   shaker. Short noise bursts through a bandpass (centre 3.2 to 4.6kHz with jitter, Q about 1.4)
   with a 2ms attack and a 22 to 35ms decay, so they read as dice not ice. Ticks accelerate with the
   phases: every 230ms, then 150ms, then 95ms, gain rising from 0.05 to 0.16. A final harder clack
   at the stop (1.9kHz, decay 80ms, gain 0.2). Silence through the hold. On reveal, one soft thock: a
   sine from 220Hz to 150Hz over 110ms at gain 0.1. The `AudioContext` is created lazily inside the
   press handler (a user gesture, so iOS allows it) and `resume()`d; `webkitAudioContext` is
   accepted; if neither exists, or the guest chose quiet, or `prefers-reduced-motion` is set, no
   sound. Master gain is modest; nothing here is loud in a bar.
4. Haptics: a new pattern `haptic('shake')` in `haptic.ts`, a rising series that mirrors the ticks
   (Android; iOS gets nothing until the reveal), then `haptic('success')` on the reveal.
5. Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`): no shake, no sound; the button reads Shaking
   for 300ms, then the reveal happens by fade alone.

### Reveal

The window's liquid clears: the `--ink` fill lightens to `--cream` over 420ms `--e-out` while the
drink name rises into it (translateY 14px to 0, opacity 0 to 1, a single 6% overshoot on the y so it
reads as surfacing through liquid, never a bounce loop). The name is `.t-strong` in `--ink` on the
now-cream window, one or two lines, 15px, centred. Long names wrap; the window's text is clipped
to two lines with the sheet's meta beneath carrying it in full.

Beneath the shaker, in this order:

- the drink's venue and deck as `p.t-meta.tnum`: **O'Malley's Irish Pub · Deck 7** (one middle dot;
  a custom drink at a user venue prints that venue's name; a drink with no venue prints the
  category alone);
- the reason as `p.t-body`, one line, from the pick (Data and state), for instance **Because you
  love gin**, **Sam loved it**, or, when the pick was random, **One you have not tried**;
- an `aria-live="polite"` region (visually the same lines) announces **Try O'Malley's Irish Pub's
  Kenmare's Kiss, Deck 7** once.

The button is enabled again, still the one coral, and reads **Go get it**. Tapping it replaces the
Shake sheet with `DrinkSheet` for that drink (the house pattern: the sheet returns
`<DrinkSheet id onClose onOpen />`, one `.sheet` at a time); closing the drink sheet returns to
this reveal, not to the page, so the guest can shake again. `Shake again` (quiet action) runs the
press sequence again immediately, from a dark window. `haptic('success')` fires once on the reveal;
no `Confirm` tick: the answer is on screen, which is DESIGN.md's own test.

### Every drink tried

If no untried drink exists on the active sailing, the shake still runs and the reveal picks the
guest's highest-rated drink (ties to the most recently logged), the reason reads **You have tried
them all. Have another.**, and the button reads **Go again**. Honest and, on a fifteen-day sailing
with 214 drinks, rare.

### The sound toggle

`Shake quietly` / `Shake with sound`, a `.quiet-action` at the foot, persisted in `localStorage`
under `spcc-shake-quiet` (read and written in try/catch as `cruises.ts` does). Default: sound on.
The label states the action, not the state, as the other quiet actions do.

### Offline, errors, empty

The catalogue is local; nothing here touches the network; no loading, offline or error state
exists and none is added. The row does not render on a sailing with no drinks (above).

## Data and state

`src/features/shake/pick.ts`, pure, type-only imports from `src/data/model` and `src/state`
shapes so it runs under `node --test`:

```ts
export interface ShakeCandidate { id: string; name: string; venue: string; category: string; spirits: string[] }
export interface ShakeInput {
  drinks: ShakeCandidate[]
  entries: Record<string, { tried?: boolean; rating?: number; date?: string }>
  forYou: { id: string; reason: string }[]   // pickedForYou, ids and reasons, in order
  recent: string[]                           // ids revealed this session, newest last
  random: () => number                       // injected, so tests are deterministic
}
export interface ShakeResult { id: string; reason: string; allTried: boolean }
export function shake(input: ShakeInput): ShakeResult | null
```

Rules, each with its reason in a comment:

- Candidates are drinks with no `tried` and no `rating`. Null when `drinks` is empty (the row is
  hidden then, so this is defence).
- Never a drink in `recent` while another candidate exists: a shaker that says the same thing
  twice running is a broken one. `recent` holds the last six ids.
- Weighting, in one pass: a candidate that appears in `forYou` gets weight 4 and carries that
  entry's reason; otherwise a candidate sharing a spirit with the guest's top spirit (ratings of 4
  or more, at least three of them, the same rule `pickedForYou` uses) gets weight 2 and the reason
  **Because you love gin** (the spirit lower-cased); every other candidate weight 1 and the reason
  **One you have not tried**. Draw by weight with `random()`. The weighting keeps the pick useful
  without making it a shelf: a random gin will still surface.
- All tried: `allTried: true`, the id of the highest `rating`, ties broken by the latest `date`,
  reason **You have tried them all. Have another.**

The sheet holds `phase: 'idle' | 'shaking' | 'revealed'`, the current result, `recent` (component
state, not persisted), and `quiet` (persisted). No store field, no persist version change, no
`partialize` change. `Math.random` is the injected `random` in the app; tests inject a fixed
sequence.

`haptic.ts` gains `shake: [12, 90, 14, 70, 16, 55, 18, 45, 20, 40, 24]` beside the two existing
patterns, with a comment saying it mirrors the rattle's acceleration.
